# System Scope | 系统边界

## 1. Research Scope | 研究范围

**EN**:
This project focuses on the operation and governance of **regulated third-party testing laboratories** under **real-world constraints**. Core concerns include:
- Data Integrity & Trust
- Lifecycle Control
- Organizational Degeneration & Anti-patterns
- Low-cost Digital Governance

**CN**:
本项目聚焦于**受监管的第三方检验检测实验室**在**现实约束条件**下的运营与治理。核心关注点包括：
- 数据可信 (Data Integrity & Trust)
- 生命周期控制 (Lifecycle Control)
- 组织失效与反模式 (Organizational Degeneration & Anti-patterns)
- 低成本数字化治理 (Low-cost Digital Governance)

## 2. Core Objects | 核心对象

**EN**:
- Small and medium-sized third-party testing laboratories
- Quality and operational units in large testing organizations
- Compliance and quality functions under highly regulated environments

**CN**:
- 中小型第三方检测实验室
- 大型检测机构的质量与运营单元
- 强监管环境下的合规与质量职能

## 3. Exclusions | 不研究什么

**EN**:
- General enterprise ERP or financial systems
- Advanced algorithms or AI models
- Pure hardware engineering or instrument R&D
- Non-regulated laboratories

**CN**:
- 通用企业ERP或财务系统
- 高级算法或AI模型
- 纯硬件工程或仪器研发
- 无监管要求的非标实验室

## 4. Purpose | 目的

**EN**:
To ensure all topics remain centered on "laboratory digital governance" and prevent unbounded scope expansion.

**CN**:
确保所有主题都围绕“实验室数字治理”这一核心，防止范围无限扩张。
