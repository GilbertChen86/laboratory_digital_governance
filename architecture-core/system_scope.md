# 系统边界

## 1. 研究范围

本项目聚焦于受监管的第三方检验检测实验室在现实约束条件下的运营与治理。核心关注点包括：数据可信、生命周期控制、组织失效与反模式、低成本数字化治理。

## 2. 核心对象

中小型第三方检测实验室；大型检测机构的质量与运营单元；强监管环境下的合规与质量职能。

## 3. 不研究什么

通用企业ERP或财务系统；高级算法或AI模型；纯硬件工程或仪器研发；无监管要求的非标实验室。

## 4. 目的

确保所有主题都围绕"实验室数字治理"这一核心，防止范围无限扩张。


# System Scope

## 1. Research Scope

This project focuses on the operation and governance of regulated third-party testing laboratories under real-world constraints. Core concerns include: data integrity and trust, lifecycle control, organizational degeneration and anti-patterns, and low-cost digital governance.

## 2. Core Objects

Small and medium-sized third-party testing laboratories; quality and operational units in large testing organizations; compliance and quality functions under highly regulated environments.

## 3. Exclusions

General enterprise ERP or financial systems; advanced algorithms or AI models; pure hardware engineering or instrument R&D; non-regulated laboratories.

## 4. Purpose

To ensure all topics remain centered on "laboratory digital governance" and prevent unbounded scope expansion.
