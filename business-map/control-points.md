|----------|-------------|

## Top 3 Priority Improvements | 优先改进的3个控制点

Based on risk and feasibility:

1. **Record form version control** – Low cost, immediate impact
2. **Audit trail enable + periodic review** – Customer-driven, builds trust
3. **Report template management** – Direct impact on client deliverables

基于风险和可行性：

1. **记录表格版本控制** – 成本低，效果直接
2. **审计追踪开启+定期审阅** – 客户驱动，建立信任
3. **报告模板管理** – 直接影响客户交付物
